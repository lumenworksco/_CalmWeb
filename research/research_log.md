# Research log (working notes; facts as read on the page named)

## S1 Bruffaerts 2018 — verified via Tilburg University research portal record
- Authors: Bruffaerts R, Mortier P, Kiekens G, Auerbach RP, Cuijpers P, Demyttenaere K, Green JG, Nock MK, Kessler RC
- J Affect Disord 225:97-103, doi:10.1016/j.jad.2017.07.044, PMID 28802728
- e-survey, n=4,921 KU Leuven freshmen, WMH-ICS
- ~1 in 3 reported 12-month mental health problems; internalizing & externalizing problems linked to 2.9-4.7% lower academic year percentage (0.2-0.3 GPA)
- Limitations (authors): sample size limited dept x student interaction analysis; no prior secondary-school performance data; causality unknown
- OA: not stated on Tilburg; Europe PMC: author manuscript PMC5846318 (free PDF https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC5846318?pdf=render)

## S2 Auerbach 2018 — J Abnorm Psychol 127(7):623-638, doi:10.1037/abn0000362, PMID 30211576, PMC6193834 (author manuscript; isOpenAccess N but free in PMC)
- 19 colleges, 8 countries (Australia, Belgium, Germany, Mexico, Northern Ireland, South Africa, Spain, US); web self-report; 45.5% pooled response rate
- 13,984 full-time respondents: 35% screened positive for ≥1 lifetime disorder; 31% ≥1 12-month disorder (abstract, confirmed in paper's abstract text)
- correlates "relatively modest"; screening (self-report), not clinical diagnosis
## S4 Lindsay 2022 — BJPsych Open 8(3):e86, doi:10.1192/bjo.2022.48, PMC9059737, CC BY. ACTUAL TITLE: "Psychological predictors of insomnia, anxiety and depression in university students: potential prevention targets". Queen's University (Canada)
- Two cohorts first-years n=1519 (2018-19), n=860 (2019-20); surveys at entry & end of first year
- Insomnia symptoms at entry predicted depression (β=0.15) and anxiety (β=0.16) at follow-up controlling for baseline (both P<0.001)
- Limitations (authors): self-report, self-selecting, younger, more female (74% v 58%), cisgender only; attrition esp. cohort 2 (COVID)
## S6 Harrer 2019 — IJMPR 28(2):e1759, doi:10.1002/mpr.1759, PMC6877279
